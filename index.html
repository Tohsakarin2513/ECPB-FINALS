About Us

Welcome to EPCB (Elijah’s Personal Computer Builds) — your trusted destination for custom PC builds, computer parts, repairs, and maintenance.

Founded in 2021, EPCB started with a simple goal: to help people find the right computer parts and build a PC that fits their needs and budget. From low-end and budget-friendly computers to high-end gaming and performance builds, we provide solutions for different types of users.

As a computer technician specializing in custom PC builds, PC cleaning, and PC repair, EPCB focuses on providing practical recommendations and quality service. We understand that choosing computer components can be difficult, especially with so many options available. That’s why our website makes it easier to explore specifications, view parts, compare different hardware, check prices, and plan your ideal PC build.

Whether you are building your first budget PC, upgrading your current system, or planning a high-end gaming setup, EPCB is here to help you make better and smarter decisions about your computer.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make PC building simple, accessible, and easier to understand by providing useful information, hardware comparisons, and reliable computer services.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted PC-building platform where anyone can confidently plan, compare, and create their ideal computer build—from entry-level systems to high-end performance machines.
